Privacy Policy — Aqim

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Aqim (“Aqim”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a prayer and Islamic spiritual habit application designed to help users build and maintain daily prayer, Quran reading, and adhkar habits.

1. No user account required

Aqim does not require users to create an account. We do not require an email address, phone number, username, password, Google account, or social-media account to use the application.

2. Location information

Aqim may request access to your device’s precise location. We use location information to determine the user’s location for prayer-time calculations and related prayer functionality. When location permission is granted, Aqim may transmit latitude and longitude to third-party prayer-time or geocoding services when required to provide these features.

You can deny or revoke location permission through Android settings. Some location-dependent features may not work correctly without location access. Aqim does not sell your location information.

3. Quran and Islamic content

Aqim provides Quran text, Quran metadata, adhkar, and audio content. Core content may be stored locally on the device and does not require an account.

4. Notifications

Aqim may send local notifications for prayer times, reminders before or after prayer, Quran reading, morning and evening adhkar, Witr reminders, and other enabled Islamic reminders. These notifications are not used to create a user profile.

5. Advertising

Aqim may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interaction information, and diagnostic information according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Aqim does not sell users’ personal information.

For information about Google’s handling of data, see Google Privacy & Terms.

6. In-app purchases

Aqim may offer digital purchases through Google Play, including a lifetime option to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Aqim does not directly receive or store complete payment card information.

Google Play’s terms and privacy practices apply to purchases made through Google Play. See Google Privacy & Terms.

7. Data storage

Aqim may store application settings and user preferences locally on the device, including prayer preferences, notification preferences, Quran reading progress, adhkar progress, habit and streak information, and location information needed for application functionality.

8. Data sharing

Aqim does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, including prayer-time services, geocoding/location services, advertising services, and Google Play services for purchases. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Data deletion

Aqim does not require a user account and does not currently provide an account-based personal-data deletion request system. Users can remove locally stored Aqim application data by uninstalling Aqim or clearing the application’s storage/data through Android settings. Information processed by third-party services may be subject to those services’ own retention and deletion policies.

10. Children’s privacy

Aqim is not specifically directed at children under 13. Aqim does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration because Aqim does not provide user accounts.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Aqim. Where information is transmitted over the Internet, Aqim uses secure network connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by the relevant service. No Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Aqim’s features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a new “Last updated” date.
